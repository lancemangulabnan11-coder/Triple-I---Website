AirFryer

Device Information

an air fryer is a kitchen appliance used to cook food with hot air. it does not actually fry food like a fryer. it works like an oven that uses air to cook your food. you can use an air fryer to cook foods like fries, chicken, and more. air fryers are extremely useful for cooking a wide range of foods.

How to Dispose

To dispose of it first you unplug the air fryer and let it cool completely. next is to clean out food and wash removable parts. Don't pour oil down the sink, let it cool, containerize it, and throw it in the trash. Remove any detachable parts (basket, tray). Check the warranty (you might get a replacement). wrap the cord and pack the unit in a box or bag. use junk removal services or town collection days if available.

How to Recycle

Take your air fryer to an electronic waste (e-waste) center so parts can be reused safely. you can use store take-back programs for appliances. if you want you can donate the air fryer if it still works or Sell it online.
