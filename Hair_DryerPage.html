HairDryer

Device Information

A hair dryer, which is also called a blow dryer is a machine that blows warm air to style or dry hair. Hair dryers help people control the way their hair looks by making it dry faster and by helping to shape it. This is because hair dryers make bonds in the hair that help it stay in place. These bonds are strong. They do not last long and can be affected by humidity. When you wash your hair these bonds go away. When people use hair dryers to style their hair they can make it look nice and neat with a lot of volume. Using hair dryers with things like special hair products, hairbrushes and combs can make hair look even better by adding hold and lift. The first hair dryer was made a time ago in the late 1800s. Then in 1912 a man named Gabriel Kazanjian made the real hair dryer model. Later in 1920 people started making hair dryers that people could use at home. Now hair dryers are used by hair stylists in salons and by people, at home to make their hair look great.

How to Dispose

Check if there is any damage parts
(exposed wires, cracks, burning smell, melted parts).
remove any damaged units from usable ones.
Pack it in a securely box or sturdy bag.
If you are disposing of multiple units, use a container with a lid to prevent it from breaking.

How to Recycle

Bring your hair dryer to a recycling center.
Donate it if it is still in good condition or still working.
make sure it is unplugged and cooled before recycling it.
Keep it intact so parts can be safely reused.
