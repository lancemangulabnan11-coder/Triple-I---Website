Introduction and Purpose of the Study

We are students from STI College Angeles from Mobile Apps and Web Development (MAWD 12A) conducting a study on the acceptability of E-Waste Hub PH. The findings of this study will serve as a basis for further enhancing the platform’s features and overall design to better serve its intended users. Given the nature of our study, we invite you to answer our survey questionnaire.

Respondent’s Information and Extent of Involvement

We have chosen Grade 11 and Grade 12 Senior High School students from STI College Angeles for the Academic Year 2025-2026 as our respondents. If you qualify and agree to participate, you will be asked to answer a short questionnaire that covers your personal background (Demographic Profile), your assessment of the platform’s features and overall functionality (Perceived Usefulness of the Platform) and your level of interest and engagement with the E-Waste Hub PH (Perceived Interest). The estimated time to complete the questionnaire is approximately ten (10) minutes.

Risk, Inconveniences, and Voluntary Participation

Given the nature of the study you will not encounter any form of risk. Expect as well that there are no personal questions in the questionnaire, though personal information such as your year level, strand, and section may be requested. Participation is completely voluntary. You have the freedom to withdraw your participation at any time without consequence. Your choice to withdraw will be met with respect and understanding.

Benefit

While there may be no immediate personal benefit to you as a respondent, your responses will greatly contribute to the development and improvement of E-Waste Hub PH. This could help developers and educators improve digital learning and entertainment systems.

Confidentiality and Data Management

Following the stipulations stated in the Data Privacy Act of 2012, all information you provide will be treated with strict confidentiality and will be used solely for academic purposes. You are entitled to ask questions you deem necessary, and you have the right to withdraw your data at any time. Data gathered will be destroyed after the completion of the study.
